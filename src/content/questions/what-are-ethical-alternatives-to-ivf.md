Use means that honor the personhood of every child: medical care that heals underlying infertility without creating embryos outside of the womb or excess embryos, or that brings unnecessary risk to your children; adoption; embryo rescue only where it does not incentivize further creation of disposable embryos; prayerful contentment in God's providence. All methods must avoid commodifying or discarding children.
